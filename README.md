# Global Superstore: Profit and Discount Analysis

A business-focused analysis of the Global Superstore dataset (51,290 order
lines, 25,035 orders, 2011-2014) built to answer one question: where does
the company make money, and where does it lose it?

## Key Findings
- Sales grew 90% and profit grew 102% over the period.
- Order lines with discounts above 20% lost 814,682, equal to 55.5% of total profit.
- The loss appears in every category and market.
- Tables is the only loss-making sub-category, but it is profitable at full
  price, so discounting is the cause, not the product.
- EMEA and Africa discount the most.
- The top 10% of customers generate 39.7% of profit.
